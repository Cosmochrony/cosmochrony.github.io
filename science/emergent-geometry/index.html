The Emergent Geometry Sub-Programme

On a supplied Weil–Heisenberg carrier, what geometric results follow from admissibility? The Carnot limit stands, and the coefficient rigidities are statements about the spin-1 module. Under the unestablished spatial limit hypothesis [H-L] and the open lifting hypothesis [H-lift], the effective co-metric has rank three; its full-rank extension and its coefficient values are open. This page is the synthesis and hub for all constituent papers.

Read the synthesis note DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20417961

Overview

The emergent geometry sub-programme asks what geometry can be reconstructed after supplying a finite Heisenberg group, its Heisenberg–Schrödinger carrier and the associated Weil action. The admissibility filter $\Pi_q$ selects modes under the Born–Infeld bounded-flux constraint. The unconditional results concern the Carnot limit and representation-theoretic coefficient rigidities; the continuum metric interpretation additionally requires [H-L].

Under [H-L] and [H-lift], the sub-programme extracts an effective co-metric from an operator rather than taking a background metric as an independent input; that co-metric has rank three, and its completion is open. It sits at the interface between the foundational branch and the physical branch (gravity, gauge, fermions), whose geometric readings inherit this open hypothesis. This note concerns metric reconstruction only; metric dynamics belong to spectral gravity.

Coefficient status. No metric coefficient has an established value. Q8 version 2.0 shows that $\mathfrak{su}(2)$-invariance fixes an invariant form only up to a free positive factor, so no coefficient is read off the Casimir eigenvalue 2, and that the commutator supplies no central coefficient; Q10 version 2.0 controls the ratio $A_H/A_Z$ only under named inputs; Q5b version 2.2 leaves the central slot empty. The value $A_\tau = 2$ asserted by Q11 and the co-metric $2\eta^{\mu\nu}$ of the presentation note are pending revision.

The structural chain

$\text{Supplied carrier} + [\mathrm{H\!\!-\!L}] + [\mathrm{ID}] \;\Longrightarrow\; \mathcal H_c = L^2(\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}) \;\Longrightarrow\; \mathrm{Heis}_3(\mathbb{R}) \;\Longrightarrow\; L_{\mathrm{eff}} \;\Longrightarrow\; g^{\mu\nu}\ (\text{rank three}).$

The final step of the presentation note, $g^{\mu\nu} = 2\eta^{\mu\nu}$, is not established: the central slot is empty (Q5b, Q8) and no coefficient value is fixed (Q8, Q10). It is pending revision.

Five conceptually distinct stages, each addressed by a distinct group of papers: the discrete-to-continuum stage, now the open spatial limit hypothesis [H-L] after the Q5a version 3.2 withdrawal (Q5a, Q5a-O2, H2); the dimensional promotion via Carnot geometry and the Bass–Guivarc'h homogeneous dimension $D_{\mathrm{hom}} = 4$ (Q5b); the metric extraction from the principal symbol of $L_{\mathrm{eff}}$, a rank-three co-metric whose non-degenerate block has signature $(-,+,+)$ (Q5b, conditional on [H-L], [H-lift] and [H-hyp]); the coefficient values, for which Q8 and Q10 establish that the Casimir fixes no coefficient, that the commutator supplies no central one, and that the ratio $A_H/A_Z$ is controlled only under named inputs, while U1 version 2.0 leaves [U] open; and the closure $A_\tau = 2 \Rightarrow g^{\mu\nu} = 2\eta^{\mu\nu}$ asserted by Q11, which is pending revision.

Papers of the sub-programme

Discrete-to-continuum limit.

  • Q5a — v3.2: exact identification of the canonical filtration (toric Fourier window), zero-form theorem, and normalisation no-go; the spatial limit is now the open hypothesis [H-L], and Q5 is open.
  • Q5a-O2 — Fourier structure of the pre-saturation admissible pipeline: pure-mode fingerprints with $o(q)$ displacements (frequencies $o(q)$ for fixed block parameters, macroscopic per-$q$ under the implemented sampling, no $q$-independent limit), and the truncation status of the three-coordinate reduction; the form is $O(q^{-2})$ on the whole unit sphere (a normalisation-prefactor fact), ruling out the $q^{-1}$ coercivity, and the hypotheses [H-E1] and [C] remain open.
  • H2 — strong convergence of the rescaled Weil generators ([H2]) via a Poisson aliasing lemma and a discrete Sobolev identity.

Dimensional promotion, metric extraction and signature.

  • Q5b — unconditional Carnot convergence of BFS shells and homogeneous dimension $D_{\mathrm{hom}} = 4$; under [H-L] and [H-lift], a rank-three co-metric $\mathrm{diag}(-A_\tau, A_H, A_H, 0)$ whose non-degenerate block has signature $(-,+,+)$ under [H-hyp]; the full-rank extension Q5b-O2 and the coefficient values are open (version 2.2).
  • Q9 — version 2.0 gives a free kinetic Mosco limit under [K] and compatible recovery [R]. It does not establish [H-F] $\Rightarrow$ [H-lift]; Q5b metric extraction still requires [H-L] and [H-lift].

The coefficient values and their status.

  • Q7 — version 2.0 states the requirements an equivariant bridge would meet and proves its rigidity under an explicit identification hypothesis $H_{\mathrm{eff}} = \mathrm{Sym}^2(V_\rho)$ that no source supplies; it determines no coefficient, and withdraws the transfer of the discrete no-cross-terms statement to the continuum symbol.
  • Q8 — version 2.0: the commutator supplies no central coefficient; a constant one needs a term of homogeneous degree four and a length scale; for a supplied fully invariant target, $A_H = A_Z = 2\lambda$ with $\lambda$ free.
  • Q10 — version 2.0: a sharp ratio lemma and a conditional bound $|A_H/A_Z - 1| \le 2\varepsilon/(1-\varepsilon)$ under [U] and three named geometric inputs; an absolute value needs an independent scale; no value derived.
  • U1 — proves an exact finite-Heisenberg-generator obstruction; version 2.0 withdraws its proof of [U] and the rate $O(q^{-1/2})$.

Metric closure and integrative output.

  • Q11 — under [H-F], $A_\tau=2$ by temporal Casimir rigidity; under [H-L] and the identification Q7 version 2.0 records as supplied by no source, this reads $g^{\mu\nu}=2\eta^{\mu\nu}$; it imports the Casimir normalisation, which Q8 version 2.0 shows fixes no coefficient, and is pending revision.
  • W1 version 2.0.1 — withdraws its former proof of [H-w]; retains a conditional bound for a distinct partial-sum proxy. The bridge to Q5a response weights and [H-w] remain open.
  • Q6b — under [H-L], assembles the conditional chain $\Pi_q\to L_{\mathrm{eff}}\to g^{\mu\nu}\to G_{\mu\nu}$ and obtains the Schwarzschild exterior and Einstein equations as consistency conditions; its co-metric $\mathrm{diag}(-2,2,2,2)$ and its Lorentzian reading are pending revision.

Inputs and outputs

Upstream inputs. A supplied finite Heisenberg group with non-trivial central character, its Heisenberg–Schrödinger representation $\pi_c$ on $\mathcal H_c=L^2(\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z})$, the identification of that carrier with the admissible fibre, and the distinct associated Weil action; the Born–Infeld bounded-flux constraint $A_n \le c_\chi/\sqrt{\lambda_n}$; and, from the spectral admissibility sub-programme (Presentation Note 1), the spin-$\tfrac12$ sector $V_\rho \cong \mathbb{C}^2$ with $\Sigma_c(n_3) = 3$ and $\mathrm{Im}\,\mathbb{H} \cong \mathfrak{su}(2)$.

Outputs. Under [H-L] and [H-lift], a rank-three effective co-metric whose non-degenerate block has signature $(-,+,+)$ under [H-hyp], with no established coefficient value, and the effective operator $L_{\mathrm{eff}}$ on $\mathbb{R}_\tau \times \mathrm{Heis}_3(\mathbb{R})$; the three spatial directions as the image of $\mathrm{Im}\,\mathbb{H}$; and, under the same hypothesis, the Schwarzschild exterior and Einstein equations (via Q6b) — consumed by the gravity, gauge and fermionic papers of Branch III.

Status

The metric reconstruction is conditional on the spatial limit hypothesis [H-L]: Q5a version 3.2 withdraws the Mosco derivation of the spatial limit operator, so establishing or replacing [H-L] is one of two leading open items, the other being the identification of the measured admissible space with the spin-1 module, which Q7 version 2.0 records as supplied by no source. Under [H-L], [H-lift] and [H-hyp], the non-degenerate block of the co-metric has signature $(-,+,+)$; the central slot needs a new operator (Q8), and no coefficient value is established. Also open: the existence of an explicit equivariant bridge, at finite $q$ or in the limit, $\phi_q: \mathrm{Sym}^2(V_\rho) \xrightarrow{\sim} W_{\mathrm{sp}}$ (the typed bridge, its uniqueness and any coefficient value depend on it), and hypothesis [H1] on the full $L^2$ space (open; Q5a-O2 delimits what the three-coordinate pipeline measures and does not close [H1], [H-E1] or [C]). This note does not address the dynamics of $g^{\mu\nu}$ — the Einstein equations are the subject of the spectral gravity sub-programme.
