Overview

Q10's deposited version proposes a route from spectral universality [U] to $\mathfrak{su}(2)$-isotropy and $A_H\to2$. This is its published claim, not a coefficient established by the current cascade. U1 version 2.0 withdraws its claimed proof of [U] and its $O(q^{-1/2})$ rate, so Q10 cannot use either as a proved input.

Q7 version 2.0 separately records the identification of the measured admissible space with the supplied spin-one carrier as open. Reading a Casimir value on that carrier as a measured horizontal coefficient requires this missing step. Q10's own coefficient argument, including its parity attribution, awaits a separate audit.

Status. The proposed coefficient identification remains conditional on open [U] and further geometric inputs; Q10 is pending revision.
