Toward Four-Dimensional Lorentzian Geometry from BFS Shell Stratification

The BFS shells of a supplied Heisenberg group give a four-dimensional Carnot geometry; a Lorentzian metric on it remains conditional and incomplete in the central direction.

Read the preprint Back to Spectral Admissibility Programme
Current Zenodo release: version 2.2 (2026-09-28). Official title: Toward Four-Dimensional Lorentzian Geometry from BFS Shell Stratification.

Overview

Q5b asks which geometric conclusions follow from the breadth-first (BFS) shell structure of a supplied finite Heisenberg group, and which require further hypotheses. Its structural result stands on its own: the BFS shells converge to a Carnot geometry of homogeneous dimension four. Its metric results are conditional.

Q5a does not derive a spatial limit operator: the published admissibility forms converge to the zero form on the canonical filtration. The spatial input of Q5b is therefore the explicit, unestablished hypothesis [H-L]. The identification of that operator with a multiple of the kinetic sector of the nonnegative operator $-\Delta_H$ is the lifting hypothesis [H-lift], which is open.

Central result. On balls of radius $n \ll \sqrt q$, BFS stratification of $\mathrm{Heis}_3(\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z})$ has a Carnot limit with $D_{\mathrm{hom}}=4$. Under [H-L] and [H-lift], the effective co-metric is $\mathrm{diag}(-A_\tau, A_H, A_H, 0)$ in the left-invariant frame: rank three, with an empty central slot. No coefficient value is established.

Core contributions

  • Carnot convergence (structural): on balls of radius $n \ll \sqrt q$, where they coincide with those of the integer Heisenberg group, the BFS shells of $\mathrm{Heis}_3(\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z})$ converge in the Gromov–Hausdorff sense to the sphere foliation of a sub-Finsler Carnot–Carathéodory metric on $\mathrm{Heis}_3(\mathbb{R})$ (Pansu, Bass–Guivarc'h). The homogeneous dimension four gives the limit the volume growth and heat-kernel decay of a four-dimensional space.
  • Metric extraction (conditional on [H-L] and [H-lift]): the effective operator $-A_\tau\partial_\tau^2 + A_H\Delta_H$ on $\mathbb{R}_\tau \times \mathrm{Heis}_3(\mathbb{R})$ has the co-metric $\mathrm{diag}(-A_\tau, A_H, A_H, 0)$ in the left-invariant frame. Its rank is three.
  • Signature (conditional also on [H-hyp]): the hyperbolicity hypothesis, applied to the sector independent of the central variable, gives the signature $(-,+,+)$ on the non-degenerate block with $\tau$ time-like; an extension $\mathrm{diag}(-A_\tau, A_H, A_H, A_z)$ with $A_z > 0$ would be Lorentzian.
  • Consistent conventions: the group law, the left-invariant fields $\tilde X = \partial_x + \tfrac{y}{2}\partial_z$, $\tilde Y = \partial_y - \tfrac{x}{2}\partial_z$, the commutator $[\tilde X, \tilde Y] = -\tilde Z$, the Schrödinger representation and the symbol convention are stated so that they agree with each other.

BFS stratification and spacetime

The emergent space is four-dimensional in two distinct senses that Q5b keeps apart: as the manifold $\mathbb{R}_\tau \times \mathrm{Heis}_3(\mathbb{R})$, with $\tau$ the projective ordering index, and through the homogeneous dimension of its spatial factor, which comes from the weights of the Carnot stratification.

What is not yet emergent is the metric in the central direction $\tilde Z$. The sub-Laplacian has no first-order part and a rank-two principal symbol, so no correction of lower order fills the central slot (Q8). A full-rank extension needs a new operator with a term $\tilde Z^2$ of homogeneous degree four, which carries a length scale.

Interpretation. The non-commutativity of the horizontal directions makes the central direction reachable but does not give it a length; the shape of the effective spacetime is structurally supported, while its full metric and its scale remain to be constructed.
