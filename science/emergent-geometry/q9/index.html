Overview

Hypothesis [H-lift] was introduced in Q5b as a conditional assumption: that the horizontal kinetic sector of the effective operator $L_\mathrm{eff}$ can be lifted to produce a positive-definite contribution $A_H > 0$ to the co-metric. The 4D Lorentzian geometry derivation of Q5b was conditional on this hypothesis.

Q9 removes [H-lift] as an independent additional assumption by proving the implication [H-F] $\Rightarrow$ [H-lift]. Under the frozen, unestablished hypothesis [H-F], the modulation-sector contribution $\mathcal{E}^X_q(f_q)$ is $\mathcal{O}(q^{-1})$ along recovery sequences, and coercivity of the effective form implies $A_H > 0$.

The implication is independent of the spatial bridge problem studied in Q7. Under [H-F], Q9 establishes $A_H > 0$; Q7 then determines the specific values of $A_{H_1}$, $A_{H_2}$, $A_{H_3}$.

Central result. [H-F] $\Rightarrow$ [H-lift]; under [H-F], the modulation position-energy contribution is $\mathcal{O}(q^{-1})$ and $A_H > 0$ follows by coercivity.

Core contributions

  • Modulation-sector suppression theorem: under [H-F] and along the relevant recovery sequences, the position-energy contribution satisfies $\mathcal{E}^X_q(f_q)\leq C/q\to0$. This is a bound on the form contribution, not on the operator norm of the unit-modulus modulation generator.
  • Coercivity of the effective form under [H-F]: once the modulation-sector contribution is shown to vanish, the effective quadratic form $\langle L_\mathrm{eff} u, u \rangle$ is shown to be coercive in the horizontal directions. Coercivity immediately gives $A_H > 0$.
  • [H-lift] as theorem: the combination of generator suppression and coercivity constitutes a proof of [H-lift]. The hypothesis is now a theorem within the Q5a framework of versions up to 2.1 — a framework withdrawn by Q5a 3.2 and consumed here as the frozen, unestablished hypothesis [H-F] (version 1.1): the result is the implication [H-F] $\Rightarrow$ [H-lift].
  • Independence from spatial bridge: under [H-F], Q9 proves $A_H > 0$ without determining the individual values $A_{H_1}$, $A_{H_2}$, $A_{H_3}$. The positivity of the horizontal sector is established separately from the equal-coefficient question addressed in Q7.
  • Reduction of Q5b assumptions: under [H-F], [H-lift] follows and need not be assumed independently. The remaining conditionality is the spatial limit hypothesis [H-L] (Q5b 2.1), which Q5a 3.2 shows to fail for the published normalisation.

Modulation generator suppression

The modulation generator $M_q$ belongs to the supplied finite Heisenberg–Schrödinger action; it is distinct from the associated Weil action. It appears in the decomposition of $L_\mathrm{eff}$ and could potentially contribute a negative term to the quadratic form, obstructing coercivity.

Under [H-F], along any relevant recovery sequence, the key quantitative result is $\mathcal{E}^X_q(f_q)\leq C/q\to0$. The $q^{-2}$ phase-oscillation factor is partially offset by the $O(q)$ discrete position spread controlled by the Born–Infeld bound, leaving the net $q^{-1}$ decay of the position-energy contribution.

As $q \to \infty$ (the continuum limit relevant for the effective geometry), the modulation position-energy contribution vanishes, and the effective form is coercive in the horizontal directions. This is the mechanism that proves [H-lift].

Quantitative bound. Under [H-F], the rate is $\mathcal{O}(q^{-1})$, not $\mathcal{O}(q^{-2})$. This suppression is sufficient for the coercivity implication.

Relation to the Cosmochrony programme

Q9 is a pivotal paper in the Q-series because it turns [H-lift] from an independent assumption into a consequence of [H-F]. It does not establish [H-F] or [H-L]:

  • Q5b: under [H-F], [H-lift] is not an independent assumption; the geometry remains conditional on the spatial limit hypothesis [H-L].
  • Q6b: [H-lift] is no longer independent once [H-F] is assumed; its geometric reading remains conditional on [H-L].
  • Q8: under the frozen, unestablished hypothesis [H-F], Q8 gives $A_Z = 2$ and Q9 gives $A_H > 0$.
  • Q10: uses the conditional $A_H > 0$ implication of Q9 as input for the complete metric determination.

Q5a 3.2 does not provide the required Mosco convergence. The current chain retains the frozen hypothesis [H-F] for the Q9 implication and the independent spatial limit hypothesis [H-L] for metric extraction.

Open directions

  • Exact $A_H$ values (Q7, Q10, Q11): under [H-F], Q9 establishes $A_H > 0$; the exact values $A_{H_1} = A_{H_2} = A_{H_3} = 2$ (conjectured from Q5b) are to be established by Q7/Q10/Q11.
  • Position-energy suppression at finite $q$: the $\mathcal{O}(q^{-1})$ bound is a limiting statement; the precise finite-$q$ corrections are relevant for numerical verification.
  • Extension to other representations: whether analogous generator suppression holds for higher-spin or other Weil representations is an open question with potential implications for the matter sector.
