Overview

W1's published version claims that admissibility weights $a_q(s)$ converge uniformly in the shell variable to a positive limit $A$, closing hypothesis [H-w] under [H1]. Its quantitative step imports an $O(q^{-1/2})$ universality rate from U1. U1 version 2.0 withdraws that rate and leaves [U] open.

W1 has not yet been revised against this withdrawal. Its fibre-invariance and summability arguments must be assessed at their own scope before the claimed uniform weight limit can be retained. The title above is the title of the current deposited W1 version, not a statement of the revised status.

Status. W1's [H-w] claim is pending audit; the imported U1 rate is withdrawn.

Claims requiring review

  • Uniformity: W1's passage from fibre-invariance of observables to uniform control of weights uses the now-withdrawn quantitative U1 input.
  • Positive limit: the separate summability argument for the candidate limit $A$ does not by itself replace the missing uniform estimate.
  • Framework closure: claims that [H-w] is closed and only [H1], [H-E1] and [C] remain open depend on that missing step.

Relation to the Cosmochrony programme

The registry records the withdrawn U1 input and marks W1 pending revision. This page describes the deposited W1 paper without treating its quantitative conclusion as established. The separate Q10 coefficient and metric claims also remain pending their own audits.
