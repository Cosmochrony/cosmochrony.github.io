Q6a Gauge Structure

Fibre symmetries, a conditional connection and the open gauge group

Read Q6aGauge structure sub-programme

Current status

Q6a defines gauge charges as functionals constant on the projection fibres, and the gauge group as the group of fibre-preserving transformations of the projection. $\mathrm{U}(1)$ is the residual phase symmetry: global phase rotations, which preserve the projection because the phase lies in its kernel. $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ is read as the minimal non-abelian admissible group under the spinor carrier supplied by O23, whose selection is open; the representation-theoretic bridge must be read with the carrier and factorisation assumptions of O27. $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ is a structural hypothesis whose identification is open.

No complete gauge group. Q6a does not use O31 and determines no complete gauge group. The colour factor, commutation, direct-product structure and the composite $G_{\mathrm{SM}}=\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(1)$ are open; O31 withdraws every derivation of them.

What Q6a establishes

  • Gauge charges as invariants of the projection-fibre equivalence classes, and the gauge group as the fibre-preserving transformations.
  • The residual phase $\mathrm{U}(1)$ and, under the supplied carrier, $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ as the minimal non-abelian admissible group.
  • Conditionally on two continuum hypotheses that it does not prove, the connection as the generator of fibre transport and the field strength as its curvature; the Yang–Mills action is a structural motivation, not a derivation from the axioms alone.
  • A conditional input interface for gauge dynamics: if a compact group and admissible connection are supplied, Q12 studies the corresponding Yang–Mills term.

What is open

Q6a determines no complete gauge group. No valid result selects $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ from colour-triplet data or proves that three sectorwise symmetries commute and form the Standard Model direct product; numerical patterns from O32 do not supply the missing identification theorem. The two continuum hypotheses, and the uniqueness of the Yang–Mills action from the axioms alone, are open.
