Temporal Residual Map from the Completed Principal Symbol: Construction, Uniqueness, and the Lorentz Identification

Constructs the temporal residual map $\mathcal{P}_\tau^{\mathrm{Q5b}}$ left open by LorentzCapacity: the unique positive function compatible with the Lorentzian splitting of a supplied co-metric, the Born–Infeld unit capacity-flow sphere and the Weil filling law. Its reading as time dilation, $\Phi(\eta_n^\tau) \to 1/\gamma$, requires the named hypothesis [H-cone].

Overview

LorentzCapacity reduces the Lorentz-factor limit of the O7 saturation parameter, under a quadratic Born–Infeld budget hypothesis, to the construction of a temporal projection $\mathcal{P}_\tau^{\mathrm{Q5b}}$ (its Lemma 1). The present paper constructs it.

Three inputs determine the map: the Lorentzian splitting of a supplied rank-four co-metric of the conditional form of Q11, $g^{\mu\nu} = \mathrm{diag}(-A_\tau, 2\lambda, 2\lambda, 2\lambda)$ with $A_\tau$ and $\lambda$ independent and fixed by no source; the Born–Infeld unit capacity-flow sphere; and the Weil filling law. The splitting uses only the signature and the decoupled temporal block, so no coefficient value enters; the normalisation $g^{\mu\nu} = 2\eta^{\mu\nu}$ is a choice of units. The explicit formula $\mathcal{P}_\tau^{\mathrm{Q5b}}\,\Sigma_n^{\mathrm{tot}} = \sqrt{1 - B_n^2}$ is proved, and each of the three inputs is individually necessary: removing any one leaves the formula undetermined.

Scope statement. The construction and the uniqueness theorem are proved under a supplied co-metric (the hypotheses of Q11 Corollary 6.1), the identification of $B_n$ with the spatial capacity flow, which is a hypothesis of the paper, and the Born–Infeld lift; hypothesis [U] enters no step. Reading the normalised load as the velocity of an observer requires [H-cone]: the light-cone speed of the co-metric equals the Born–Infeld bound per ordering step. This is the coupling datum that Q11 leaves open, and no source derives it.

Main result

The temporal residual map is determined explicitly:

$\mathcal{P}_\tau^{\mathrm{Q5b}}\,\Sigma_n^{\mathrm{tot}} = \sqrt{\Sigma_n^{\mathrm{tot}}\bigl(2-\Sigma_n^{\mathrm{tot}}\bigr)} = \sqrt{1-B_n^2}$

Consequence: $\eta_n^\tau = B_n/\mathcal{P}_\tau^{\mathrm{Q5b}}\Sigma_n^{\mathrm{tot}} \to \beta\gamma$ and $\Phi(\eta_n^\tau) \to 1/\gamma$. Under [H-cone], $\beta$ is the velocity of the observer in units of the light-cone speed, and this is time dilation.

Key contributions

  • Explicit formula: $\mathcal{P}_\tau^{\mathrm{Q5b}}\Sigma_n^{\mathrm{tot}} = \sqrt{1-B_n^2}$ (proved under the stated hypotheses).
  • Uniqueness: the three inputs (Lorentzian splitting, BI sphere, Weil filling law) are individually necessary; removing any one leaves the formula undetermined (proved).
  • Failure analysis: explains the numerical failure of all three candidates tested in LorentzCapacity.
  • Lemma 1 of LorentzCapacity: established under the same hypotheses.
  • [H-cone] named: the identification of the light-cone speed with the Born–Infeld bound, on which the kinematic reading rests.

Structure

  1. Introduction — the open construction of LorentzCapacity and the scope of each input.
  2. Setup — the supplied co-metric of the form of Q11 Corollary 6.1 with two independent coefficients, the quadratic budget hypothesis, and [H-cone].
  3. Capacity-flow bundle — the unit Born–Infeld sphere and the identification of $B_n$.
  4. Construction and uniqueness — the explicit formula from the three constraints.
  5. Necessity — each input is necessary; failure of the LorentzCapacity candidates.
  6. Lorentz identification — $\Phi(\eta_n^\tau) \to 1/\gamma$, read as time dilation under [H-cone].
  7. Logical status — status of every input, and of LC-O1 to LC-O3.

  • Q8: $A_H = A_Z = 2\lambda$ with $\lambda$ free for a supplied invariant form; no value of $A_Z$.
  • Q10: no coefficient value is derived.
  • Q11: the conditional rank-four form $\mathrm{diag}(-A_\tau, 2\lambda, 2\lambda, 2\lambda)$ (Corollary 6.1), with $A_\tau$ and $\lambda$ independent; the modelling identification $\tau = n\,\Delta\tau$ with a free step; the coupling datum is open (Q11-O1).
  • U1: hypothesis [U] is open; it is not used.
  • Born–Infeld paper: the capacity bound [A-cap]; the quadratic budget is a hypothesis of LorentzCapacity.
