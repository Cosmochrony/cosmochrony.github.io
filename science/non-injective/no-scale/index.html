No External Dimensional Scale at the Level of Admissibility

This note claimed in version 1 that no independent dimensional scale can enter the admissibility constraints. Version 2.0 withdraws that theorem, records the question as open, and keeps the dimensional observations that hold.

Read the preprint Back to the foundation track
Current Zenodo release: version 2.0 (2026-11-11). Official title: No External Dimensional Scale at the Level of Admissibility.

Overview

Version 1 stated a Level 1 theorem: any deformation of the admissibility constraints that introduces a dimensional parameter independent of the Born–Infeld constant \(c_{\mathrm{BI}}\) (written \(c_\chi\) elsewhere on this site) breaks spectral scaling, structural non-injectivity or bounded admissibility closure. A corollary excluded Reissner–Nordström and Schwarzschild–de Sitter exteriors.

Version 2.0 withdraws that theorem, its corollary and that application. Three things are kept apart:

  • The proof is invalid: its premises are not in the cited papers, and each of its cases has internal gaps.
  • The claim is withdrawn.
  • The question is open: an invalid proof does not show that every Level 1 property is false.
Scope statement. The note proves no rigidity theorem. It keeps dimensional observations under explicit premises. Level 1 and the stronger Level 2 (every scale determined by \(c_{\mathrm{BI}}\) and the cascade) are both open.

Why the proof fails

  • Axioms: the bound and a unique primitive scale were attributed to Foundation's A3 and A4. In Foundation, A3 is non-premature selection and A4 is projection locking; both refer to the capacity saturation threshold, but neither states the amplitude bound or a single-scale condition, or excludes a dimensional parameter, and no source cited by version 1 states that \(c_{\mathrm{BI}}\) is the unique primitive scale.
  • Depth-dependent deformations: the argument used a power law for the pair capacity \(\sigma_{\mathrm{pair}}\) determined by the admissibility structure, which O24 does not state (it reports a finite-window fitted exponent), and a continuum limit, whereas Q5a proves that the admissibility form converges to the zero form and leaves the spatial limit open. Even granting a power law, an unbounded factor such as \(\log n\) keeps the exponent.
  • Configuration-dependent deformations: a weight on configurations does not make the projection injective; excluding it needs a premise that weights are constant on fibres, which ENI does not state.
  • Reissner–Nordström / Schwarzschild–de Sitter: the application was internally inconsistent. If \(c_{\mathrm{BI}}\) had the dimension of a non-zero power of length, \(\Lambda\) would not be independent; if not, the Schwarzschild radius would be excluded on the same ground.

What the note keeps

  • The dimension of \(c_{\mathrm{BI}}\) is not fixed by the model. The capacity axiom \(|\partial_\tau\phi_v|\le c_{\mathrm{BI}}\) gives \([c_{\mathrm{BI}}]=[\phi][\tau]^{-1}\), and O20's derivation of the bound \(A_n \le c_{\mathrm{BI}}/\sqrt{\lambda_n}\) gives, conditionally on a second-order oscillatory sector, \([A_n]=[\phi]\) and \([\lambda_n]=[\tau]^{-2}\), but neither \([\phi]\) nor \([\tau]\) is assigned. The white paper reads \(c_{\mathrm{BI}}\) as a speed, which the current axiom does not import. The note records this gap and does not choose a dimension.
  • Independent dimensional parameters, relative to an assignment: a parameter is independent if its dimension is not a rational multiple of \([c_{\mathrm{BI}}]\) under a supplied dimensional assignment; this is adequate only when the dimensions of the spectral data are multiples of \([c_{\mathrm{BI}}]\), in particular when they are dimensionless. With independent base dimensions of length, time and mass, at most one of \(G\), \(\hbar\) and a mass scale has a dimension that is a multiple of \([c_{\mathrm{BI}}]\).
  • Homogeneity observation: if the bound's right-hand side is a quantity of the amplitude's dimension, a constant factor depending only on a parameter whose dimension lies outside the span of the bound's ingredients cannot make the bound depend on that parameter, and a dimensionless factor rescales \(c_{\mathrm{BI}} \mapsto c_{\mathrm{BI}} f(\lambda)\). Parameters entering through depth-dependent combinations are not excluded.
  • Gram rigidity: that classification is character-theoretic and scale-free; the earlier statement that it is derived under the Born–Infeld bound is withdrawn.

Interpretation

This is a reading, not a result. The programme's working reading is that physical scales should arise as outputs of the projection and of the spectral structure rather than as primitive inputs. The note does not establish that reading, and the geometric sector already carries scales not derived from \(c_{\mathrm{BI}}\): Q6b lists a full-rank extension's length scale, the free temporal step of Q11, and the Einstein and cosmological coefficients as matching data.

Relation to the Cosmochrony programme

This note belongs to the non-injective foundations track, alongside Foundation M and the ENI no-go theorem. It no longer restricts downstream constructions: the programme currently has no result available that excludes an independent dimensional parameter from the admissibility constraints.

Open directions

  • A dimensional assignment: state the physical dimensions of the objects of the model, without which no statement on independent parameters can be applied.
  • Depth-dependent deformations: which deformations of the filter change the large-depth behaviour of \(\sigma_{\mathrm{pair}}\), and by what law.
  • Fibre-constant weights: whether any principle of the programme imposes them.
  • Level 2: whether every emergent scale is a functional of \(c_{\mathrm{BI}}\) and the spectral cascade.
