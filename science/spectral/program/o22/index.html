Projection Locking of the Saturation Depth: A Discrete Admissibility Theorem under Born–Infeld Saturation

O22 follows O21 by proving that, within its stated model, a realisable Born–Infeld saturation depth lies on a BFS shell. It does not establish the O21 shell-alignment conjecture, which concerns the observable rank and remains open.

Read the preprint Back to Spectral Admissibility Programme

Overview

This article continues the spectral admissibility programme after O21. O21 built a two-point crossing rank \(n_3^{\mathrm{obs}}\), free of fitted parameters for a fixed normalisation of the observable, and conjectured that physical admissibility requires this rank to lie close to a BFS shell.

O22 proves a statement about a different object, the Born–Infeld saturation depth \(n_{\mathrm{sat}}\). It introduces the projectively admissible shell support \[ \mathbb{N}_q, \] and shows that, under its definition of physical realisability, the continuous Born–Infeld saturation locus \[ L_{\mathrm{BI}} \] can be realised only where it meets this discrete support.

The integer value of \(n_{\mathrm{sat}}\) follows from that definition, which requires a saturation event to be encoded on a BFS shell, together with a shell decomposition of the observable space. Theorem 4.8 does not establish that such a depth exists.

Version 1.19. The former Corollary 5.1, which recovered the O21 shell-alignment conjecture, is withdrawn. Its proof identified \(n_3^{\mathrm{obs}}\) with \(n_{\mathrm{sat}}\) by citing an O21 proposition that only shows independence from the choice of reference shells. Nothing relates the O21 threshold \(9/q^2\) to the Born–Infeld saturation value, and \(n_3^{\mathrm{obs}}\) depends on the normalisation of the observable. The conjecture remains open. The abstract no longer states that the pair observable governs the charged-lepton mass hierarchy: that comparison is a research motivation, not a result.

Core contributions

  • Discrete admissibility support: definition of the projectively admissible shell set \[ \mathbb{N}_q, \] with observable space taken to decompose as \[ O = \bigsqcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}_q} O_n. \]
  • Continuous saturation locus: the observable is treated as a discrete sampling of an effective decay law \[ \tilde{\sigma}(t) \sim C\, t^{-\delta_{\mathrm{pair}}}, \] so the Born–Infeld condition defines a continuous candidate saturation depth.
  • Projection locking theorem (Theorem 4.8): under the definition of physical realisability, a realisable saturation depth lies in \(L_{\mathrm{BI}} \cap \mathbb{N}_q\), hence on a BFS shell.
  • Internal chain: the result is conditional on a supplied saturation scale, whose value [A-cap] does not determine, \[ c_\chi \to A_n^{\max} = \frac{c_\chi}{\sqrt{\lambda_n}} \to L_{\mathrm{BI}} \to L_{\mathrm{BI}} \cap \mathbb{N}_q \to n_{\mathrm{sat}} \in \mathbb{N}. \]
  • Withdrawn corollary: the former recovery of the O21 shell-alignment conjecture is withdrawn; the logarithmic commensurability condition of O21 is not recovered either.

Interpretation

O22 separates two objects that earlier versions conflated: the Born–Infeld saturation depth \(n_{\mathrm{sat}}\), which the paper places on a shell within its model, and the observable rank \(n_3^{\mathrm{obs}}\) of O21, whose alignment with a shell remains conjectural.

Within the model, saturation is an admissible realisation event constrained by the discrete support of \(\Pi\), not merely a value crossing, as encoded in the definition of physical realisability.

Relation to the Cosmochrony program

The sequence reads: O16 (pair observable), O17 (pair dynamics), O18 (parity covariance; fibre identification open), O19 (canonical normalisation), O20 (persistence criterion), O21 (crossing rank free of fitted parameters, for a fixed normalisation), O22 (projection locking of the saturation depth).

The shell-alignment conjecture of O21 is not closed by O22.

Open directions

  • Shell selection: derive which shell the saturation depth selects, not merely that it lies on one.
  • Shell alignment of the O21 rank: any relation between \(n_3^{\mathrm{obs}}\) and \(n_{\mathrm{sat}}\); a large-\(q\) campaign over many conjugate pairs would test the O21 conjecture, not Theorem 4.8.
  • Threshold: \[ \Sigma_c(n_3)=3 \] remains a supplied selection rule; reading it as three physically stable directions is an interpretive hypothesis.
  • Universality: test projection locking beyond Heisenberg graphs.
  • Transfer closure (settled negatively): the continuation \[ \delta_{\mathrm{pair}} \to \beta^* \] is no longer an open deliverable: the span-growth no-go proves it has no derived carrier on the Heisenberg measurement substrate, so the numerical agreement with the charged-lepton window stands only as a cross-substrate phenomenological check.
