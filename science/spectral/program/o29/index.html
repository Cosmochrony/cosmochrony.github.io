What is established

On the stated O25/Q5a checkpoints, symmetric exterior-square data satisfy three quadratic relations and exhibit a hard $6\to3$ collapse. The measured complex trajectory span is denoted $W_{\mathrm{adm}}\subset H_{\mathrm{eff}}\cong\mathbb C^3$.

The constraint forms generate $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ and act irreducibly on this three-dimensional measured space. These are direct statements at the level of the data.

Typing correction. The leading two PCA directions define an auxiliary space $U_c^{(2)}\subset H_{\mathrm{eff}}$. It is not $V_\rho$, has no canonical representation status, and does not measure $d_\rho=2$, $d_\rho^2=4$, or a Veronese square root.

The “locking-broken” stages B and D reach the maximal operator rank $4=\dim\operatorname{End}(U_c^{(2)})$ inside the auxiliary truncation. This records recovery of four operator directions there. It does not identify a spin-$\tfrac12$ representation.
