No test of $[\mathrm{H\text{-}color}]$. Colour triplets, conjugate pairs and arbitrary sets of characters are indistinguishable by the campaign's observables. The hypothesis is neither confirmed nor refuted; it is not testable with this observable.

Central result

Each O12 fingerprint vector is a single Fourier character $e_B$ with frequency $B=c_1b_1+c_2b_2+c_3b_3$, and the rank increments that define the capacity profile count distinct frequencies. Multiplying the whole character block by any unit $\lambda$ multiplies every frequency by $\lambda$, a bijection of $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$, so the profile does not change.

Under the sampling law of the campaign, the profile attached to a character therefore has the same distribution for every character: no $O(q^{-1})$ correction and no role for cube roots of unity. The version-1.5 variance ratio, the relation $\delta_{\mathrm{tri}}\approx3\delta_c$ (an identity of least-squares fitting) and the rank of $C_{\mathrm{color}}$ all have laws that do not depend on which characters are compared.

Null controls

  • $q=61$: mean variance ratio $1.28\times10^{-2}$ for the four colour triplets, $1.32\times10^{-2}$ for twenty arbitrary triples.
  • $q=151$: $3.74\times10^{-3}$ for the colour triplets, $3.51\times10^{-3}$ for the arbitrary triples.
  • The stored version-1.5 campaign is reproduced exactly, and its calibration is rebuilt on shells unaffected by rank saturation.

Withdrawn from version 1.5

  • The claim to test $[\mathrm{H\text{-}color}]$ and the verdict attached to $R\leq3$.
  • Proposition 1 and the averaged and effective levels; the levels attributed to O31.
  • The noise floors and ratios $R$, which mixed two averaging conventions.
  • The $\mathrm{SU}(3)$, quark, baryon, confinement and $\beta^*$ readings.

Open work

A test of a colour orbit needs an observable that is not invariant under all units. No such observable is proposed here.
